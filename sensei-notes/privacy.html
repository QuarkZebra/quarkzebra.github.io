YOUR WORK STAYS YOURS

Privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026 · Sensei Notes for iPhone and iPad

Sensei Notes does not collect your notebook contents or track your activity. No Sensei Notes account is required.

Notebooks and PDFs

The app reads and edits documents you choose. Notebook files contain the original PDF, handwriting, text and other objects you add. They are saved on your device or with the storage provider you select through Apple’s Files interface. The app does not upload them to a server operated by Matthew Brown.

If you use iCloud Drive, Google Drive or another file provider, that provider handles storage, syncing and any related data according to its own privacy policy. Exporting or sharing a file is an action you choose.

Preferences stored on your device

Sensei Notes stores settings, such as appearance and drawing preferences, locally using Apple’s standard preferences system. These preferences are used to operate the app and are not sent to the developer.

No advertising or analytics

The app includes no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs or account service. It does not collect your contacts, location, advertising identifier or usage history. Apple may process App Store and diagnostic information under its own privacy settings and policies.

Support requests

If you email support, the email address, message and attachments you choose to send are received and used to respond to your request. Email and forwarding providers process those messages as part of delivery. Please avoid sending sensitive information, student records or private notebook contents. You can request deletion of your support correspondence by contacting the address below.

This website

This support site is hosted using GitHub Pages and the domain’s infrastructure providers. They may process standard web request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site. These pages do not add analytics, advertising scripts or cookies.

Keeping and deleting your files

Your notebooks remain in the locations you choose until you remove them. Manage or delete them using Files and your storage provider’s controls, including any Recently Deleted folder or backups. Removing the app does not necessarily remove copies held by a cloud provider or exported elsewhere.

Children and classrooms

Sensei Notes does not require a profile or collect personal information from users through the app. Teachers and families should use their own school and storage-provider rules when saving or sharing documents containing personal information.

Changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. Sensei Notes is developed by Matthew Brown. For privacy questions or support, email support@mathbrown.com.
