Sensei Notes

Room to write.
Tools to make the maths clear.

Write on PDFs, sketch ideas, and work with editable shapes, axes and functions. Keep your notebook for the next lesson, or export a PDF to share.

Start with a page

Open a PDF

Choose a PDF in Documents, then tap Save editable notebook. Sensei Notes creates a .snote file and leaves the original PDF unchanged.

Write or read

On iPad, write with Apple Pencil. Turn Pencil only off to use your finger. On iPhone, View only starts on; turn it off when you want to edit.

Keep the editable copy

Use the .snote file for future edits. Export PDF makes a separate, flattened copy for printing or sharing.

A few useful answers

Where are my notebooks saved?

In the location you choose in Files: on your device, iCloud Drive, or another enabled storage provider. Sensei Notes does not run its own cloud storage service.

Can I use Google Drive?

Install Google Drive and enable it under Locations in the Files app. Save your notebook there. Close the notebook and wait for syncing to finish before opening it on another device. Sensei Notes does not merge simultaneous edits.

Why can’t I write on my iPhone?

Turn off View only at the top of the notebook. This switch starts on each time a notebook opens, so you can browse without adding accidental marks.

Can I erase writing already printed in a PDF?

New handwriting and objects added in Sensei Notes are editable. Marks that were already flattened into the imported PDF remain part of its background.

How do I turn pages and zoom?

Use the arrows or page number in the bottom bar. Pinch to zoom. On iPad with Pencil only enabled, one-finger swipes turn pages and two fingers pan the page.

What should I do if a file won’t save?

Keep the notebook open. Check that your storage provider is connected and has space, then try Save again. If a notebook changed on another device, resolve the file conflict before continuing. Keep separate backup copies of important work.
